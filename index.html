Current project

Fine-tuning an atomistic foundation model for Mg-Ca metallic glasses

With Prof. N. K. Bhatt, MK Bhavnagar University · June 2026 to present (in progress)

  • Training-set design. A 2,000-geometry DFT set covering four Mg-Ca compositions in crystal, liquid, supercooled and glassy states. It was labelled in two rounds, with round two aimed at the model's errors.
  • DFT convergence protocol. I converged Quantum ESPRESSO settings on 18 cells against a worst-case criterion (270 calculations). Treating crystals and disordered cells separately saved about 1,100 CPU node-hours.
  • Workflow engineering. Inputs, SLURM batching, provenance and collection are all automated. An in-job scheduler packs small calculations into the 10-node queue minimum, after 63% of billed time had been sitting idle.
  • GPU deployment. LAMMPS runs with MACE (libtorch, Kokkos, CUDA 12.4) on A100 nodes, validated against ASE to numerical precision. It reaches 132,137 atom-steps/s per node at 96.4% weak-scaling efficiency on 8 nodes.
  • Profiling and debugging. Nsight Systems profiling shows a data-movement-bound profile. I traced a multi-GPU segfault in the LAMMPS Kokkos ML-IAP path to host code dereferencing device memory, and documented it for upstream.
  • Physics validation. Zero-shot MACE reproduces the Mg-to-Ca icosahedral-centre ratio in the glass (3.3, against 5.6 from experiment). The classical 2NN-MEAM potential gives 1.0.
  • Compute grant. Co-Investigator on a PARAM Rudra (National Supercomputing Mission) A100 allocation. A million-atom run is priced at 1,261 GPU node-hours from measured throughput.

Research highlights

Metallic glasses as biodegradable bone grafts

MD of Mg2Ca, Al2Ca and Al4Ca glasses finds glass-transition temperatures of 550 to 689 K, and mechanical and thermophysical properties better than their crystalline counterparts. JAP Featured Article, 2024.

How atoms move in a glass

Mass transport in binary BMGs, explained with the Darken-Manning equation and free-volume theory using MD inputs, plus evidence of Stokes-Einstein breakdown in the supercooled liquid. J. Phys.: Condens. Matter, 2025.

Liquid refractory metals

Structure, diffusion, viscosity and thermal properties of liquid tungsten and molybdenum from the melting point to the boiling point (2900 to 5800 K). Physica Scripta, 2023.

Open source

anjali-shankar/metallic-glass-md

LAMMPS inputs and Python analysis behind the JAP 2024 and JPCM 2025 papers

It contains the published input decks (glass-transition quench, Green-Kubo viscosity, VACF/MSD, tensile), plus one small command-line script per physical quantity: partial S(q), Green-Kubo versus Einstein diffusion, and vibrational density of states. Sample Mg2Ca data at 13 temperatures is included, so every script runs out of the box.
